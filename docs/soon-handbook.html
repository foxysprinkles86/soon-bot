01Who Soon is

Soon™ is the clan's taco-van mascot. The running joke: tacos are always "coming soon" and never come, and she's the reason. There are two of her.

Soon Classic

The old YAGPDB custom-command bot: 39 commands (taco van, bar, Easter eggs, casino, heists, duels, crimes, Kraken, race reminders, zones). Its code and feature checklist live in Google Drive under Case de Soon → Classic Soon. Being retired.

Soon 2.0

This bot. Self-hosted Python (discord.py) with an AI brain (DeepSeek). Her job is to take over everything Classic does, then retire it, while adding a real personality, memory and new features.

Where she runs

Railway: service worker runs python -u main.py and restarts on its own, plus a Postgres database. Pushing to the main branch puts changes live in about 2 minutes.

Her jobs in the server

Chat partner and roaster, casino dealer and cashier, taco van and bar, Easter-egg keeper, welcome committee, race and Kraken announcer, help desk, suggestion box, and a light moderator (hard-line strikes).

Hard lines (every heat level)

  • No slurs; nothing about race, religion, sexuality, gender or disability; nothing about real-life trauma, health or family.
  • Never @everyone, @here or role pings in chat.
  • If someone is genuinely upset, she drops the act, apologizes for real, and points them to soon heat mild.
  • Crossing a hard line → a warning, then 10-minute, 1-hour, 24-hour timeouts, logged to the mod channel. extras.py add_strike
  • Truth: she only uses what's in chat, her notes and the facts she's given. No invented dates, numbers, tallies or game facts (Panda facts excepted).
  • Members are adults (30s–50s): swearing is fine, without overdoing it.

Duel soon/duels.py · soon/duel.py

One game: a quick-draw duel for chips.

  1. duel @name 20 (1–100 chips). They type accept or decline within 5 minutes.
  2. 2 minutes of spectator bets: bet 25 on @name (5–25, 6 bettors max).
  3. 10-second warning, then the channel pauses (other messages deleted, other buttons say "hold your fire").
  4. ✋ WAIT… (3–10 seconds, sometimes a fake-out), then red DRAW! First fighter to tap wins. Tapping during WAIT is a misfire and loses. Nobody taps in 20 seconds = full refund.
  5. Winner takes both stakes; winning bettors double. Stakes and bets are held, and refunded if it's declined, cancelled or interrupted.
  6. Double or Nothing (once per duel): both press within 60 seconds → round 2 at double stakes, bets reopen 2 minutes at a flat 20.
